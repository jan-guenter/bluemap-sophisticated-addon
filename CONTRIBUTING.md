# Contributing

Open an issue before expanding the supported artifact set or visual scope.
Compatibility is exact-profile data, not a version range.

Contributions must be independently written under MIT. Do not copy or adapt
Sophisticated source, classes, models, textures, translations, or captures.
Third-party artifacts may be inspected privately to establish identifiers,
formats, and behavioral facts, but they must not be committed or published.

Keep Storage and Backpacks as sibling routes. A fault in either route must
return that route to BlueMap's original resource rendering without disabling
the other. Frequently changing contents, counts, fill levels, energy, module
activity, animations, and held/displayed items are outside scope.

Submit one complete change and rely on the pull-request gate rather than
repeating full builds after every small edit. The authoritative gate is listed
in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
